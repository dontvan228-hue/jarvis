Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Jarvis is a personal voice assistant that runs on its owner's Windows computer. It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. By using Jarvis you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Data Jarvis accesses

With your permission (the youtube.upload scope) Jarvis uploads video files that you placed in the Videos\YouTube folder on your computer, together with the title, description and tags for them. Jarvis does not access any other YouTube or Google account data.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • Videos and their metadata are sent only to YouTube, only when you ask Jarvis to upload them.
  • The Google access token is stored only on your computer (%USERPROFILE%\.jarvis\youtube\token.json). A local list of uploaded file names and video IDs is kept in the same folder so the same file is not uploaded twice.
  • Jarvis has no server, collects no analytics and does not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties.
  • Voice commands are recognised on the computer; the text of a request may be sent to the AI model that powers the assistant (Anthropic Claude) to understand it. YouTube data is not included in those requests.

Cookies

Jarvis does not use cookies and does not store information on or read it from your browser.

Deleting your data and revoking access

You can revoke Jarvis's access at any time on the Google security settings page. To delete the locally stored data, delete the %USERPROFILE%\.jarvis\youtube folder. Jarvis stores nothing else, so nothing remains after that.
