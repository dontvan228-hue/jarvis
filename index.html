Jarvis

A personal voice assistant for Windows, used by a single YouTube creator on her own computer.

What it does

Jarvis listens for voice commands and performs simple actions on the computer: opening programs and websites, setting reminders, changing the volume, answering questions.

It can also upload a finished video to the owner's own YouTube channel when she asks for it by voice ("upload the video to YouTube"). The video must already be in a dedicated local folder (Videos\YouTube). Jarvis suggests a title, description and tags and uploads the video through the YouTube Data API.

How YouTube data is used

  • The only permission requested is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload.
  • Jarvis only calls videos.insert to upload a video the owner chose.
  • Videos are always uploaded as private. The owner reviews each one and publishes it herself in YouTube Studio.
  • Jarvis does not read, change or delete existing videos, comments, analytics or any other channel data.
  • There is no server: everything runs on the owner's computer, and no data is sent anywhere except to YouTube.

Who can use it

Jarvis is not a public service. It is a personal tool for the owner of one YouTube channel; it is not offered to other users.

Jarvis uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
